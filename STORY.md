# Essen — The Shadow Born of Iron

*Story bible for the game. Structure lives in `lib/story/story.dart`; all text (English, Azerbaijani, Japanese) lives in `lib/l10n/story_*.dart`.*

---

## Premise

Hōmura Province, in the age of the warring states (Sengoku period).

Twenty-one winters ago, a foreign trading ship, the **Morgenrot** ("morning-red"), broke apart on the black rocks of the Kurogane coast during a typhoon. One survivor was carried away from that beach: an infant wrapped in a cloth stitched with a single foreign word — **ESSEN**.

The shinobi of **Kasumi-gakure**, the Village Hidden in Mist, raised the child as one of their own. Now that child is a grown shinobi. One night he comes home from a mission and finds his village burning.

The game's theme: **what makes a person belong somewhere?** Is it blood, a name, an oath, or the people who chose you? Every major character carries a single night they are ashamed of, and the story asks whether one night defines a life.

## Characters

### Essen — the protagonist
- Twenty-one years old, a shinobi of Kasumi-gakure.
- Born to **Heinrich**, a master smith from the city of Essen, and his wife **Margarethe**, both foreigners. He doesn't know this when the game begins.
- His pale, sea-grey eyes earned him the nickname "little ghost". He has always been the outsider who had to work twice as hard to belong.
- Quiet, precise, dryly funny with the few people he trusts. His great fear is that he belongs nowhere.
- **Arc:** from *revenge* ("he will learn what a shadow costs") to *choice*. In the end he decides what kind of hand he will be.

### Master Kiriya Sōun — the mentor
- Master of Kasumi-gakure and Essen's adoptive father. Patient, fond of cranes and bad poetry.
- **Secret:** he was once Masakage's finest samurai. On the night of the wreck he cut down three of Heinrich's sailors, and was then ordered to kill the infant. He couldn't do it. He deserted, carried the baby through the storm, and spent twenty-one years atoning in the mist.
- He dies in Chapter 1 before he can confess. His truth reaches Essen in pieces, from other people.

### Hotaru — the sworn sister
- Daughter of Kasumi's headman, the finest kunoichi of her generation: sharp-tongued, fearless, loyal.
- She was the first child to call Essen "brother", and broke the nose of the first one who laughed at it.
- Captured during the raid to bait Essen. Even as a prisoner she leaves him a trail of notes, and while Essen fights Masakage she frees the enslaved forge workers.

### Lord Tetsuryū Masakage — the antagonist
- Daimyō of the **Tetsuryū (Iron Dragon) clan**.
- Brilliant, patient and without mercy. He believes the warring states will be won by iron and fire, not courage.
- On the night of the wreck he took Heinrich's journal of steel-making and had every survivor killed. For twenty-one years his secret forge has failed: his musket barrels crack. The final pages of the journal were sewn into the infant's swaddling cloth.
- When he learns the child lives in Kasumi, he burns the village.

### General Takeshi Rendō — the rival
- Masakage's general, called "the Unbent Blade". He has never struck a fleeing man or broken his word.
- **Secret shame:** at seventeen he held a torch on that beach and did nothing. He saw Sōun refuse the order.
- He duels Essen at the Sakura Shrine because his oath requires it. When Essen spares him, he chooses his eyes and his tongue over his sword. He reveals the truth and keeps his men away from the north gate.

### Obaa Tsuru — the witness
- A fisherman's widow of Hanamura, past eighty.
- She sheltered Sōun and the infant on the night of the wreck and kept the secret for twenty-one years.
- She tells Essen where he came from, and gives the story its moral centre: *"A man is more than the night he is ashamed of."*

## World — the Kurogane Valley

| Place | Description |
|---|---|
| **Kasumi-gakure** (west) | The ruined Hidden Mist village. Burned houses, a broken watchtower, the ruined dojo, the memorial stone. |
| **Bamboo forest war camp** (centre-west) | The Iron Dragon camp choking the roads: war curtains (maku), red nobori banners, the commander's tent. |
| **The river** | Winds through the valley from north to south. Two vermilion bridges cross it, and it is shallow enough to wade. |
| **Hanamura** (north-east) | The Village of Flowers: thatched farmhouses, rice paddies, a small torii, Tsuru's house. |
| **Sakura Shrine** (east hill) | A five-storied pagoda, the main hall, and a path of five torii gates lined with stone lanterns and cherry trees. |
| **Iron Dragon Keep** (south hill) | A walled castle with corner turrets, a north gatehouse, a multi-tier keep, and the courtyard where Hotaru is caged. |

## Plot

### Chapter 1 — Ashes of the Mist (霞の灰) · *Night*
Essen returns from a mission to find Kasumi in flames, with Iron Dragon samurai looting the dead. He clears them out (tutorial: stealth, Shadow Strikes, shuriken, smoke). He finds Master Sōun dying by the ruined dojo.

Sōun tells him that Masakage came for **him**, that Hotaru was taken as bait, and gives him the cloth: *"Your mother stitched those letters."* He tries to confess something about "that night on the beach" and dies before he can.

### Chapter 2 — Whispers in the Bamboo (竹の囁き) · *Dawn*
Essen tracks the raiders to a war camp in the bamboo forest, silences the guard and steals the commander's orders:

> *"The kunoichi is to be delivered alive. The foreign child will follow her — let him. The forge must be fed by the new moon. General Rendō holds the shrine road."*

Essen realises he is being herded, and that he is "the foreign child".

### Chapter 3 — Petals of Steel (鋼の花びら) · *Day, cherry blossoms*
In Hanamura, Essen drives off the tax collectors and meets Obaa Tsuru. She tells him about the wreck of the Morgenrot, his parents, Masakage's massacre, and the samurai who saved him: **Kiriya Sōun, wearing the Iron Dragon crest**.

Essen follows **Hotaru's three hidden notes** along the road. They reveal the secret forge, the enslaved villagers, the cracking musket barrels, and that the last secret is in Essen's cloth. The last note says: *"This is a trap. Do not come for me. …But you will come."*

At the shrine, **General Rendō** duels Essen and yields. Spared, he confesses his own shame from the beach, confirms that Sōun saved the child, and tells Essen that Hotaru will be executed at dusk.

### Chapter 4 — The Iron Dragon's Keep (鉄竜の城) · *Sunset*
Essen breaks through the gate guard and frees Hotaru, who goes to free the forge slaves. Masakage confronts him.

At half health, Masakage reveals the last poison: Sōun personally killed three of Heinrich's sailors before he "grew a conscience". Essen answers: *"Then he spent twenty-one years paying for it. What have you paid?"*

Masakage is defeated and the player must **choose**.

## Endings

### Mercy — "The Gentle Hand" (優しき手)
Essen spares Masakage. Rendō binds his own lord in chains for the Shogun's judgment. Inside the cloth Essen finds his father's letter and the furnace drawings. He keeps the letter and burns the drawings. Kasumi is rebuilt with a memorial carrying two names side by side: **Kiriya Sōun** and **Heinrich of Essen**.

### Vengeance — "The Shadow's Justice" (影の裁き)
Essen kills Masakage, and the Keep burns for three days. He burns both the drawings and the unread letter. He helps Hotaru rebuild Kasumi, then vanishes into legend: a light-eyed shadow who appears when the strong prey on the weak.

### Heinrich's Letter (codex, unlocked at the end)
> *"My son — steel is neither good nor evil. It is only the hand that holds it. If you ever read this, be a gentle hand. Your father, Heinrich."*

## Ideas for future chapters
- **The Shogun's Court:** Masakage's trial (mercy ending) or the clan's revenge (vengeance ending).
- **The Morgenrot's Wreck:** dive the ship and find Margarethe's journal and the truth about why Heinrich came to Japan.
- **Rendō's Path:** a playable side story as the general rebuilding his honour.
- **Hotaru's Night:** a prequel chapter of the raid, played as Hotaru.
